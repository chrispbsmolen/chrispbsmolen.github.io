The Covering Array Table

The covering array tables maintained by Charles Colbourn list the best known sizes and point to the construction behind each, but do not include the arrays themselves, so the arrays could not be downloaded and checked directly.

I built and maintain the open table that starts from that state and adds the arrays submitted and checked since, along with published bounds from the literature, for strengths 2 to 6, alphabets up to 25 symbols and up to 100,000 columns. Every entry names its source, and 1,045 arrays are on file so anyone can download and check them.

The R package CAs on CRAN loads the table at start-up, and its contributing guide sends new arrays here.

New results, checked exactly

Cyclic covering perfect hash familiesReport, Zenodo

Nine covering arrays of strength five and six smaller than the best sizes previously recorded, and a tenth by fusing a symbol. Two checkers, written separately, check each of the nine exactly, up to 1.23 × 1014 column sets for one family.

Spherical codesHenry Cohn's table

The table at spherical-codes.org flagged 4,252 codes as needing more optimization. I improved 3,650 of them and certified every one exactly. The site credits me on the entries where my codes are still the best known.

The growth constant of total dominating sets of the square grid, computed to fourteen stable digits, with new terms a(31) to a(38) of OEIS A133793 and checkers that share no code with the computation.
